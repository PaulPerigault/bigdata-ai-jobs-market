# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The summary below is the working rule.

## Our standard

Be respectful and constructive. Welcome questions, give precise and kind reviews, accept criticism of
your work without taking it personally, and focus on what is best for the project and its users.

Not acceptable: harassment or discrimination of any kind, insults or personal attacks, sexualised language
or attention, publishing someone's private information, and any conduct that would be inappropriate in a
professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, code review) and whenever someone
represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that break these rules, and may ban a contributor
temporarily or permanently. To report a problem, use GitHub's private
[vulnerability reporting form](https://github.com/PaulPerigault/bigdata-ai-jobs-market/security/advisories/new)
(it reaches the maintainer privately) or contact the author through [paulperigault.fr](https://paulperigault.fr).
Reports are handled confidentially.
